❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your Umrah journey

📝 Booking & Payment

How do I book an Umrah package?
You can book through our website using the Instant Quote Calculator for quick pricing, or submit a Custom Enquiry for personalized packages. You can also call us on 020 8840 6420 or visit our Ealing office.
What deposit is required?
A deposit of £200 per person secures your booking. The remaining balance is due 8 weeks before departure. For Ramadan bookings, we may require a higher deposit due to limited availability.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfer, debit cards, credit cards (small fee applies), and cash payments at our office. PayPal is also available for online bookings.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes! After your initial deposit, you can spread the remaining balance across multiple payments leading up to your departure date. Just let us know when booking.

📦 Packages & Inclusions

What's included in the package price?
All packages include: return flights, hotel accommodation, visa assistance, airport transfers in Saudi Arabia, and meals as specified (breakfast for 3★, half-board for 4★, full-board for 5★). See Packages for full details.
What's the difference between 3★, 4★, and 5★ packages?
  • 3★ Economy (from £899): Hotel 400-500m from Haram, breakfast only
  • 4★ Comfort (from £1,099): Hotel 100-200m from Haram, breakfast + dinner, Ziyarah tours
  • 5★ Premium (from £1,475): Hotel steps from Haram, all meals, train travel, group leader + religious guide
Do you offer Ramadan packages?
Yes! Ramadan 2026 falls in February/March. We offer special Ramadan packages with a 25% seasonal premium. Book early as these sell out quickly. See our February 2026 page.

🛂 Visa & Documents

Do I need a visa for Umrah?
Yes, UK passport holders need a Saudi Umrah visa. We handle the entire visa application process for you. You'll need a valid passport with at least 6 months validity and recent passport photos.
What documents do I need?
  • Valid UK passport (6+ months validity)
  • Recent passport-sized photos (white background)
  • Completed visa application form (we'll help with this)
  • Proof of relationship for women under 45 traveling with mahram
Can women travel alone for Umrah?
Women aged 45+ can travel without a mahram in groups. Women under 45 must travel with a mahram (male relative). Contact us to discuss your specific situation.

✈️ Travel & Hotels

Which airports do you fly from?
Most packages depart from London Heathrow (LHR). We can also arrange departures from Gatwick, Stansted, Manchester, and Birmingham on request.
How close are the hotels to Haram?
All our hotels are within walking distance of Masjid al-Haram. 3★ hotels are typically 400-500m, 4★ hotels 100-200m, and 5★ hotels just steps away (50-100m).
How do we travel between Makkah and Madinah?
3★ and 4★ packages use comfortable air-conditioned coaches (4-5 hours). 5★ Premium packages include the Haramain High-Speed Train (2.5 hours). You can upgrade to the train for an additional fee.

🛡️ Protection & Safety

Is my booking ATOL protected?
Yes, all our packages are ATOL protected, meaning your money is secure if the company fails before or during your trip. You'll receive an ATOL certificate with your booking confirmation.
What happens if I need to cancel?
Cancellation fees depend on how close to departure you cancel. More than 8 weeks: deposit lost. 4-8 weeks: 50% of total. Less than 4 weeks: 100% of total. We recommend travel insurance to cover unexpected cancellations.

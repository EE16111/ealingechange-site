Ultimate Umrah Packing Checklist

Don't forget the essentials. A complete list for a comfortable journey.

👕

For Men

  • ✓ Ihram (2 sets recommended)
  • ✓ Ihram Belt / Money belt
  • ✓ Safety pins for Ihram
  • ✓ Comfortable walking sandals
  • ✓ Unscented soap & deodorant
  • ✓ Hair clippers/razor (for Halq)
🧕

For Women

  • ✓ Loose, non-transparent Abayas
  • ✓ Hijabs / Scarves / Caps
  • ✓ Comfortable walking shoes (Sketchers etc.)
  • ✓ Unscented toiletries
  • ✓ Small excessive bag (crossbody)
  • ✓ Hair scissors (for Taqseer)

🎒 General Essentials

Documents & Electronics

  • • Passport (Valid 6+ months)
  • • Visa Printout/Phone Copy
  • • Flight Tickets & Hotel Vouchers
  • • Power Bank (20,000mAh recommended)
  • • Universal Travel Adapter (UK/Type G works mostly)
  • • Saudi Sim (buy on arrival or use eSim)

Health & Comfort

  • • Vaseline/Sudocrem (for chafing/rashes)
  • • Paracetamol & Painkillers
  • • Throat Lozenges / Cough syrup
  • • Imodium/Diarrhea medication
  • • Rehydration sachets (Dioralyte)
  • • Sunglasses & Hat (for day trips)

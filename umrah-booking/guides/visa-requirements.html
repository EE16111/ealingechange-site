Complete Umrah Visa Guide for British Citizens (2026)

📅 Updated Jan 22, 2026 • ⏱️ 5 min read

Planning your spiritual journey? The first step is getting your Visa sorted. Here is everything UK passport holders need to know about the new rules.

1. The Tourist Visa Waiver (EVW)

British citizens can now perform Umrah on a standard Tourist Visa Waiver. This is the most popular and cheapest option.

  • Cost: Approx £30-£40.
  • Validity: Single entry, up to 6 months.
  • Processing: Instant online.

⚠️ Important:

This visa is for British Passport holders ONLY. If you hold a travel document or other nationality, rules differ.

2. The Dedicated Umrah Visa

You might need a specific Umrah Visa if:

  • You are travelling with a group that requires it (rare now).
  • You are not eligible for the Tourist Waiver.

3. Requirements

To apply for any visa to Saudi Arabia, you need:

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months.
  • Recent passport-style photo.
  • Confirmed flight booking (sometimes asked).

Historical Ziyarat Guide

Sacred sites in Makkah and Madinah that connect you to Islamic history.

🌴 Madinah Al Munawwarah

🕌

Masjid Quba

The first mosque built by the Prophet (PBUH). Offering 2 Rakaat here carries the reward of an Umrah.

⛰️

Mount Uhud

Site of the Battle of Uhud. "Uhud is a mountain that loves us and we love it." Visit the Martyrs' cemetery.

🕋

Masjid Qiblatain

The mosque where the revelation to change the Qibla from Jerusalem to Makkah was received.

🪦

Jannat Al-Baqi

The main cemetery of Madinah where many family members and companions of the Prophet (PBUH) are buried.

🕋 Makkah Al Mukarramah

Jabal Al-Nour (Cave of Hira)

The mountain housing the Cave of Hira, where the Prophet (PBUH) received the first revelation (Iqra). It requires a steep hike to reach the top.

Jabal Thawr

The cave where the Prophet (PBUH) and Abu Bakr (RA) took shelter during their migration (Hijrah) to Madinah.

Jannat Al-Mualla

The historical cemetery in Makkah where the Prophet's (PBUH) wife Khadijah (RA) and other ancestors are buried.

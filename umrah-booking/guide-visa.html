Umrah Visa Guide for UK Citizens (2026)

Everything you need to know about entering Saudi Arabia for Umrah.

Tourist eVisa vs Umrah Visa

Feature Tourist eVisa (Most Popular) Umrah Visa
Eligibility UK, EU, US Citizens All Nationalities
Validity 1 Year (Multiple Entry) 90 Days (Single Entry)
Processing Instant / 24 Hours 2-5 Days
Restrictions None (Can visit all cities) Mostly Religious Purposes

📋 Requirements for UK Citizens

  • Passport with at least 6 months validity from entry date.
  • Digital passport-style photo (white background).
  • Credit/Debit card for visa fee payment.
  • Basic travel details (though flight booking not strictly required for eVisa).

💰 Estimated Costs

Tourist eVisa: Approximately 450-500 SAR (~£100-£120) including mandatory insurance.

Umrah Package Visa: Usually included in our package prices or charged separately depending on the season.

⚠️ Important Note on Rawda

While the visa allows entry, visiting the Rawda (Riaz ul Jannah) in Masjid An-Nabawi requires a separate appointment via the Nusuk App. Booking in advance is highly recommended.
